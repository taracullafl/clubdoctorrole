What is the Role of the Club Doctor & MDSD in a Mandatory Notification?

Mandatory Notification Club Doctor Role MDSD Role
Removal Process

Tend to the player as clinically indicated

Remove the player from field (HIA Removal Process available if required)

Assist in the activation of the HIA Removal Process if required
Video Review

Review the video (separately or together)

If any Category 1 signs are observed directly or on video review, this is consistent with a clear diagnosis of concussion requiring immediate removal & no return to play
Clinical Assessment

Perform a Detailed Off-Field Assessment (including SCAT6)

Perform a Detailed Off-Field Assessment (including SCAT6) if requested by the Club Doctor
Discussion Required

Club Doctor & MDSD must discuss the video review & results of the SCAT6 to determine the outcome of the assessment (unless the MDSD is tending to another, more serious, injury.)
Documentation Requirement

Complete HIA form in the SCORE App

Complete comments section of SCORE App using one of following outcomes:

  • Concussion diagnosed
  • Player cleared & allowed to return to play
  • Player removed from play unanimous decision not reached
  • Player removed from play for other reasons
